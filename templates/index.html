Phishing Protection Tips

🔍

Check the domain

Look carefully at the domain name. Attackers often use domains that look similar to legitimate websites.

🔒

Look for HTTPS

HTTPS encrypts communication between your browser and the website.

⚠️

Avoid suspicious links

Be careful with unexpected links asking for passwords, payments or personal data.
